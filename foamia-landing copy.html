FAQ

Questions owners usually ask

Do I need new hardware to use Foamia?+
No. Foamia runs on any phone or tablet — owner, staff and customer apps all work on devices you already have.
Can I manage more than one wash centre?+
Yes — the owner dashboard shows every centre you run, with the option to drill into one location at a time.
What payment methods does Foamia support?+
UPI, cards and cash are all supported at checkout, and every payment is logged automatically to your daily report.
How long does setup take?+
Most centres are live within a week. Our onboarding team helps you add your bays, services and staff on a setup call.
