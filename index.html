FAQ

Questions car wash owners ask

Can't find your answer? Talk to our team.

Does Foamia handle oil changes as well as washing?

Yes. Car washing and oil change jobs run in the same app — you can even add both to one job and one invoice. Each car's oil change history is saved, so your team always knows what was done last time.

What do I get — an app or a dashboard?

Both. Your staff use the Foamia mobile app (iOS and Android) to check in cars, update jobs and take payments. You and your managers use the ERP dashboard in any web browser to see revenue, invoices, branches, staff and stock. Everything stays in sync in real time — no new hardware needed.

How does Foamia stop cash going missing?

Every payment is entered against an invoice by a named staff member. You can see who collected what, when and at which branch — so the day's totals always add up.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. Branch management lets you run every location from one owner login and compare jobs, invoices, payments and stock branch by branch.

Can I control what each staff member can see?

Yes. With user management, every staff member gets their own login and role — owner, manager, cashier or technician — and only sees what their role allows.

How does stock management work?

Keep track of your oil, filters, shampoo and other supplies at each branch, so you always know what you have and what to reorder.
